category: recipes
---
## Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon white sugar
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon butter or margarine
- ½ cup milk

## Steps

1. Gather the ingredients.
2. Stir together flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
3. Cut in butter until mixture is crumbly. Stir in milk and mix until a batter forms that is thick enough to be scooped with a spoon.
4. Drop batter by spoonfuls into boiling stew or soup. Cover and simmer without lifting the lid for 15 minutes.
5. Serve hot and enjoy!

## Yield

6 servings

## Total Time

20 mins
