A short, throwaway post with no image and no notes — just here to confirm that a freshly dated file shows up in the right places: the Notes category index, its sidebar preview, the Archive, and the Latest feed, each with today's date attached correctly.

No explicit `date:` in the front matter, on purpose — the filename's own date (`2026-08-31`) is what Jekyll uses, which is the pattern every other post here follows after the earlier bug where an explicit same-day timestamp got silently excluded by Jekyll's future-post filter.
