This is the first article on nomonum.

Articles like this one live in the `_posts` folder — name each file `YYYY-MM-DD-your-title.markdown` and Jekyll takes care of the rest. Write in plain Markdown: headings, links, lists, and images all work as you'd expect.

Photos live separately, in `_photos`, and show up full-width on their own page, and alongside articles in whatever category they share.
